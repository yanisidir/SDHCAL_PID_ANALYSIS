# PID-Aware Energy Reconstruction

This directory contains studies of the coupling between particle identification and energy reconstruction.

## Purpose

Hadron energy reconstruction can depend on the particle hypothesis. These scripts compare scenarios where reconstruction is performed without PID information against scenarios where PID-informed choices or particle-specific models are used.

## Contents

- `pi-_proton/`: pion/proton coupling studies.
- `kaon_pi-_proton/`: three-category coupling studies.
- `../results/pid-energy-coupling/summary/`: summary confusion matrices used in the coupling analysis.

## Typical Outputs

- linearity and relative-deviation plots;
- relative-resolution plots;
- CSV summaries for selected reconstruction scenarios;
- comparisons between no-PID and PID-informed reconstruction.

These studies should be read as exploratory internship analysis outputs, not as final detector-performance claims.
